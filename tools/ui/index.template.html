career‑copilot · worklist

Roles come straight from company applicant-tracking systems — Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, Workday. Nothing here applies, submits, or fills anything on your behalf.

Internships and co-ops are listed in their own section and are never counted as full-time openings. They are kept apart because they are a different job with a different offer — and kept at all because some new-grad pipelines run through them.

“New” here means new to this list: the date this system first saw a posting, not the date the employer published it. Both dates are shown, and they routinely disagree by months — a company added to the watchlist today arrives with all of its still-open roles.

There is deliberately no match percentage. A role is either eligible or it is not — eligibility is pass/fail and is never scored — and the fit score is only ever shown as the set of things the posting named, counted against the posting's own list.
